# Allen Institute Brand Guidelines — Reference

## Core Principle
"We are what we say and how we say it." Voice stays consistent while flexing across audiences, intentions, and touchpoints. Themes: **open science, foundational research, dynamic connections, journey, accelerators**.

---

## Logotype & Icons

### Logotype
- Word forms seen: `alleninstitute/`, `allen institute/`, stacked `allen / institute/`
- **Always trailing forward slash.**
- Stacked lock-up used **rarely**, only when space is limited.
- Apply logotype horizontally or vertically.

### Lens icon (the "a/" lens — primary profile icon)
- Zooms in on the `open` icon; articulates **focus, discovery, revelation, identification**.
- Always used as the profile icon; often locked up with the logotype.
- Render in any core palette color, ensuring legibility on background.
- **Cropping/bleed:** bleed off one or two edges (right or top edges preferred). **Never bleed more than ~25% of the icon off the page.** Used to inject energy/dynamism.
- **As container:** lens can contain images or video to spotlight content/process. **Do not place portraits inside the lens.**

### Open icon
- Most reductive brand mark, formed from the `a` + forward-slash of the logotype.
- "Condensation symbol" — used **rarely**, only for formal/refined applications and certain merchandise.
- Generally rendered in **neutral colors only**.

---

## Forward Slash `/`
- Articulates **both connection and separation**; signals more content to follow.
- Represents the accelerators; emphasizes impact and dynamism.
- Evolves directly from the breadcrumb naming approach.

### Breadcrumb hierarchy (naming convention)
Use forward-slash separated breadcrumbs for hierarchy and journey:
- `allen institute/brain science/cell types`
- `allen institute/search`
- `attribution and structure/everything is a journey`
- Nav style: `about / people / research / open science / careers`

## Typography

Custom typeface family: **Allen Institute**. 5 weights × roman + italic. Two editions: **Headline** and **Text**.

### Text edition (below 24pt)
- Designed for legibility/clarity at small sizes.
- Distinguishing features: curved tail `l`, lower stroke contrast.
- **Wider letter spacing — never alter.**

### Headline edition (above 24pt)
- **Tightly letter spaced.**
- Higher stroke contrast; brings personality.

### Roman weights available
light / medium / bold

### Italic weights available
light italic / regular italic / bold italic

### Type hierarchy (as illustrated on the cheat sheet)
| Role | Style |
|---|---|
| `section/` | Large, bold (display weight) |
| Headline | Bold |
| Sub-head | Semi-bold |
| Articulated text | Regular, includes punctuation marks `! ? @ + ( * )` |
| Numbers / emphasis | Italic, e.g. `0123456789` |

## Imagery

### Image direction
- Curate iconic images of three subjects: **people, science, place**.
- **Always crop and position dynamically** within the frame (no centered/static compositions).
- **Increase contrast levels** on all images.

### Image treatment
- Treat graphically via **luminance** or **colorization** (e.g., monochrome wash in a brand color).
- Goal: generalized thematic feel, not specific story-telling — creates a consistent visual language.
- Headlines reversed out of gradations are acceptable when contrast supports legibility.

### Infographics
- Simple, dynamic, engaging.
- Bold percentage callouts are a signature pattern (e.g., large `46%`, `91%/9%` split figures).

## Iconography
Icons should be:
- Simple
- Understandable
- Linear
- Geometric
- Scalable

Style on the cheat sheet shows monoline geometric icons rendered in a single accent or neutral color, sometimes inside a circular badge.

---

## Communication Structure
- All comms share the same building blocks and visual language.
- Stories scale up/down across touchpoints — same system, different sizes.
- The `360°` motif signals joined-up, omnidirectional messaging.

---

## Quick Application Checklist

When producing a Allen Institute–branded asset:

1. **Lock-up:** logotype with trailing `/`; lens icon present and in a legible palette color.
2. **Hierarchy:** name/path uses breadcrumb with forward slashes.
3. **Type:** Allen Institute Headline >24pt (tight tracking) or Text <24pt (default tracking — do not change).
4. **Color split:** primary on backgrounds, base for factual content, accents for highlight only.
5. **Image:** dynamic crop, contrast pushed, optionally luminance/colorization treated.
6. **Lens-as-container:** OK for images and video, **never for portraits**.
7. **Cropping the lens:** off right or top edge, ≤25% bleed.
8. **Open icon:** reserve for refined/formal use; neutral colors.
9. **Iconography:** linear, geometric, single-color.
